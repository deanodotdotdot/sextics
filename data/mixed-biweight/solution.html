A Jacobian primitive for the asymmetric mixed biweights

Construction

Put R=X_2^2, v=Y_0/X_0, c_1=X_0Y_1/(X_1Y_0), and c_2=X_0Y_2/(X_2Y_0). The squared coordinates give the plane cubic

U=ax^3+by^3+cz^3+dx^2y+ey^2z+fx^2z,

where a=1+v^6, b=1+c_1^6v^6, c=1+c_2^6v^6, d=c_1v^2/2, e=c_1c_2v^2/2, and f=c_2v^4/3. Thus F=R^3U(X_0^2/R,X_1^2/R,1), with source A=v^3R^3x^3.

The Hessian covariants give a map to the cubic's Weierstrass Jacobian. Lean uses integral covariants scaled by (3,9,27); the map coordinates \xi=\Theta/H^2, \eta=-J/(2H^3) retain their classical values. CubicSyzygy checks the defining identity, and CubicFieldDifferential checks its differential. With T=Rv^6, the Jacobian coefficients are polynomials in R,T. The degree (2,4,6) section in JacobianSeed has an invertible thirteen-variable incidence Jacobian modulo five. Lifting this seed supplies a generically finite parameterization. Polynomial integration and trace along a finite field extension produce the required rational residue primitive.

Certificate

PhysicalPrimitive transports that primitive back to the exact sextic, including the moving-map and normal-chart corrections. The irreducibility and nonvanishing conditions are established in PhysicalField and the coordinate helpers. Shared residue clearing and homogenization then give a nonzero homogeneous denominator D, six numerators N_i of degree \deg D+1, and a polynomial Q, with \gcd(D,F)=1, satisfying

AD^2-D\sum_iN_iF_i+ F\left(D\sum_i\partial_iN_i-\sum_iN_i\partial_iD\right)=F^2Q.

Solution binds this existential certificate to the protected target. No explicit expanded witness is exported.

The construction adapts hodgepodgeconjecture's cycle-three proof, including its classical covariant normalization following Tom Fisher and the SageMath authors. It uses the maintained lifting, trace, transport, and clearing constructions of fqlx and the Hodge Computer collaborators, together with Mathlib.
