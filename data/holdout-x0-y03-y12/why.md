The target
\[
A=X_0\cdot Y_0^3\cdot Y_1^2
\]
shifts two degrees from the initial even coordinate of the central cubic into the middle odd coordinate. It lies in the conjugate character sector to that central target under the holdout's diagonal symmetries. This makes it a useful example of extending the construction across characters, rather than merely varying a monomial within one character space.

Among the 58 paired-sign-invariant primitive sextic directions, the direct quotient constructions support a 48-dimensional subspace. The ten remaining directions split into six and four in conjugate residue characters. This target lies in the group of four. A character includes the determinant of the volume form as well as the numerator's transformation, so numerator invariance alone does not settle the question. In particular, the endpoint exchange fixes this numerator and reverses its residue.

The factorization \((X_0\cdot Y_0^3)\cdot Y_1^2\) suggests a binary construction with a multiplier, but \(Y_1\) is involved in both mixed terms of the sextic. Any such field must handle the multiplier in its derivative and divergence equations. A relation between abstract character spaces does not provide that rational field or a coordinate substitution between target representatives.

The [central holdout construction](#case/holdout) supplies a possible bridge across these character spaces. Its auxiliary Fermat cover and moving-conic family provide primitives for polynomial sources on a weighted quotient, rather than just for the original central monomial. That general input theorem is established, but the physical source and derivative identities still have to be supplied for a new target.

Here the middle odd-coordinate factor interacts with the exchange used on the cover. A successful specialization must account for it in the source before taking the deck trace; it cannot be appended as an inert multiplier afterward. Recovering the conjugate character also requires checking the unwanted trace term explicitly.

Compare [\(X_0\cdot X_1^4\cdot Y_0\)](#case/holdout-x0-x14-y0). Both targets lie in the same four-dimensional residual component, but the redistribution between even and odd coordinates changes the input to the quotient calculation. The pair tests the distinction between a common geometric support mechanism and the separate density identities needed for each class.
