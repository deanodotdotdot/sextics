The [coupled Fano construction](#case/reflection-coupled-fano) supplies an explicit weighted quartic, a nonsingular deformation system, and a polynomial primitive on the quotient by \(Y_2\mapsto-Y_2\). Here the sextic is unchanged, but the target is \(A=X_0^3\cdot Y_0^3\), which is even in \(Y_2\). The example tests the target boundary of a successful construction on exactly the same geometry.

The residue volume changes sign under the reflection. The served target \(X_0^3\cdot Y_2^3\) is odd, so its residue descends; the present target has the opposite residue character. Equivalently, the map \(u=Y_2^2\) has Jacobian density \(2Y_2\). Changing the polynomial source on the quotient changes the even factor multiplying that density, but cannot produce this target. The [reflection theorem](#technical-information/geometric-support) therefore gives no existence conclusion for the selected class.

The quartic and its finite-family integration remain useful information. They settle the quotient's primitive problem and identify precisely the component still missing from the original fourfold. One can ask whether a correspondence involving the branch locus, a different finite cover, or a construction with nontrivial reflection character transfers support to this complementary component. Simply reusing the quotient primitive with a different source leaves the density identity unsatisfied.

Exact curation checks rule out a linear direct-sum decomposition, the subset Euler formulas, and serving junior quotients among the projective diagonal symmetries. The sole coordinate reflection is the one just discussed. These checks concern specified mechanisms; they do not exclude hidden nondiagonal geometry or an unrelated denominator. Smoothness follows from the same good reduction as for the companion. The shared real slice makes the two targets directly comparable, but supplies no evidence about their certificates.

The challenge is to cross a character boundary after the curve-finding and integration steps are already understood. It asks for the unrestricted certificate, with no imposed denominator degree, and does not assert that nonexistence is plausible.
