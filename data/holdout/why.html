The central holdout isolates what happens when two adjacent couplings are added to the Fermat sextic:

X_0^3\cdot X_1\cdot Y_0\cdot Y_1 +X_1^3\cdot X_2\cdot Y_1\cdot Y_2.

They form an oriented chain through the three coordinate pairs, while the target A=X_0^3\cdot Y_0^3 is concentrated at its first end. The equation is sparse and retains substantial symmetry, but the separated-block constructions no longer apply directly.

In particular, simultaneously changing the signs of X_i,Y_i preserves the equation, and exchanging X_2,Y_2 is a reflection. That reflection fixes the target numerator and reverses the residue volume, placing this class outside its invariant residue sector. The collection of direct reflection and diagonal-quotient arguments serves 182 of the 208 primitive odd sextic directions; this target is among the ten missed directions within the 58-dimensional paired-sign sector. These are limits of those particular quotient arguments, not limits of geometric existence.

The equation also has an instructive surface fibration over the ratios [X_0:Y_0], [X_1:Y_1]. On a ratio chart, quotienting the two remaining pair scales by their signs gives a degree-six surface in \mathbb P(2,2,1,1); its generic smooth resolution is a K3 surface. This organizes the geometry without providing a primitive compatible with variation over the whole base. A K3 fiber is not an obstruction to every possible supporting divisor on the fourfold.

The target therefore asks for support beyond the sectors reached by these direct quotients. One possibility is to find useful symmetry on an auxiliary variety and descend its contribution; another is to construct a rational field directly on the coupled equation. Either route must recover this particular residue class, including the determinant of any coordinate change and the derivatives of its parameters. Recognizing a simpler fiber alone does not discharge those conditions.

The companion targets keep the equation fixed while varying the numerator or pole order. They distinguish a construction for one class from a mechanism serving a whole character space. The central cubic is a focused starting point: it has minimal variable support and a sparse defining equation, yet the two couplings already defeat the separated-block formulas of Chapter 2.
