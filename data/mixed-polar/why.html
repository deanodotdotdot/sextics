The target is the balanced monomial

A=X_0\cdot X_1\cdot X_2\cdot Y_0\cdot Y_1\cdot Y_2,

on a deformation of the holdout with chain coefficients 1/2 and an additional term \tfrac13\cdot X_0\cdot X_1\cdot Y_0\cdot Y_1^3. That term has bidegree (2,4), whereas the chain terms have bidegree (4,2). The paired sign changes preserve both equation and target.

Compare the identical target on the all-six holdout instance. Its cover construction uses T^6=Y_0^6+Y_2^6, y=Y_1, and the exchange y\leftrightarrow T. In the present equation the extra coupling becomes

\frac13\cdot\alpha\cdot X_0\cdot X_1\cdot y^3\cdot T, \qquad \alpha=Y_0/T.

It lacks the exchanged term proportional to y\cdot T^3. Consequently the equation does not descend to the same weighted quotient, even though the balanced target has exactly the trace character used successfully on the holdout. A favorable target projection cannot compensate for the loss of the quotient geometry on which its primitive is constructed.

The complementary quotient of Chapter 2, §2.8 has a different failure here. Its diagonal action with weights (5,1,5) on Y gives the extra coupling weight two. Consequently the polynomial-density integration theorem on that quotient cannot be imported without first constructing a replacement quotient.

The common factor X_0\cdot X_1\cdot Y_0\cdot Y_1 in the first coupling and the added term still leaves useful structure. It suggests investigating adapted quadratic coordinates or a replacement cover, with the asymmetric combination of X_0^2 and Y_1^2 kept explicit. A successful construction must control both the equation and the density; matching the target's pairwise balance alone is insufficient.

The paired-sign substitution Y_i=r_iX_i, u_i=X_i^2 makes another boundary explicit. The added coupling becomes \frac13r_0r_1^3u_0u_1^2, reversing the first cubic edge. The cyclic cubic construction shows that a Jacobian and a moving-map correction can replace the lost chain quotient, but its six-coefficient cyclic cubic and polynomial-curve incidence are not this model. Here both the altered Jacobian family and the balanced physical source need a derivative-compatible transport. The shared moving-map identity addresses a correction term once such data are supplied; it does not furnish the missing primitive.
