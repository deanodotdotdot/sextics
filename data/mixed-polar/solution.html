A reverse-edge Jacobian construction for the balanced mixed-polar target

Exact target and construction

This is an existence proof for the immutable triple

\begin{aligned} F={}&X_0^6+X_1^6+X_2^6+Y_0^6+Y_1^6+Y_2^6\\ &+\frac12X_0^3X_1Y_0Y_1 +\frac12X_1^3X_2Y_1Y_2 +\frac13X_0X_1Y_0Y_1^3,\\ A={}&X_0X_1X_2Y_0Y_1Y_2,\qquad q=1. \end{aligned}

It extends the cyclic and transitive Jacobian constructions to the cubic with a reversed first edge. The target-specific ingredients are its covariants and first variations, a nonsingular polynomial-curve incidence, and the transport of the balanced source. Arithmetic lifting, polynomial integration, finite trace, and homogeneous clearing use the maintained shared constructions.

The formal contract is the algebraic certificate statement associated with Diamond's general GHC reformulation, as described in the project overview. The submission proves that statement for the displayed triple and exports an existential theorem.

Work in the function field of the affine cone over F=0. Put

R=X_2^2,\qquad v=Y_0/X_0,\qquad u=\frac{Y_1/X_1}{v},\qquad w=\frac{Y_2/X_2}{v}, \qquad x=X_0^2/R,\quad y=X_1^2/R.

Then

F=R^3U(x,y,1),\qquad A=uwv^3R^3xy,

where

U=ax^3+by^3+cz^3+dx^2y+ey^2z+fxy^2

and

\begin{aligned} (a,b,c)&=(1+v^6,\;1+u^6v^6,\;1+w^6v^6),\\ (d,e,f)&=(uv^2/2,\;uwv^2/2,\;u^3v^4/3). \end{aligned}

PhysicalCoordinates establishes these identities in the exact residue field. PhysicalField proves irreducibility over \mathbb C by a monic Eisenstein specialization, constructs that field, and supplies its tangent derivations.

The cubic, its Jacobian, and the balanced variation

Use the Weierstrass normalization

\eta^2=\xi^3+I_4\xi+I_6,

with

I_4=-3acef+cd^2e

and

I_6=-\frac{27}{4}a^2b^2c^2-a^2ce^3 +\frac92abc^2df-ac^2f^3-bc^2d^3 +\frac14c^2d^2f^2.

The covariants in CubicCovariants, with their syzygy proved in CubicSyzygy from the exact certificate data in CubicIdentityData, give

Writing g=U_x(x,y,1), and letting D be the relative cubic derivation with Dy=1, Dx=-U_y/g, and all six cubic coefficients constant, their invariant-differential identity is

gD\xi=6\eta,\qquad \frac{D\xi}{2\eta}=\frac3g.

from CubicPoint. This gives a legitimate field-valued map with nonzero H,J,g.

The balanced source calls for the first variation

U_\tau=U+\tau xyz.

In the implementation, cubicJet is U+2kxyz, so this variation has k=\tau/2. At z=1, keep y and the coefficients fixed and move

\dot x=-xy/g.

This preserves the cubic equation to first order and gives

\dot g=y-xyU_{xx}/g,\qquad \dot I_4=\frac{9abc-cdf}{2},\qquad \dot I_6=acde^2.

PhysicalCubicJets computes these variations in dual numbers at the fixed equation. Both the curve identity and the invariant-differential identity hold to first order there. The shared moving-map identity consequently yields

\frac{\dot g}{g} =\frac{\dot I_4\xi+\dot I_6}{2\eta^2} -\frac g3D\!\left(\frac{\dot\xi}{2\eta}\right).

The derivative term accounts for the motion of the cubic-to-Jacobian map and becomes an explicit divergence correction below.

Polynomial-curve incidence on the Jacobian

Set

T=Rv^6,\qquad X=R^2\xi,\qquad Y=R^3\eta.

The Jacobian equation becomes

V=Y^2-X^3-A_4(R,T)X-B_6(R,T)=0,

where

A_4=R^4I_4\big|_{v^6=T/R},\qquad B_6=R^6I_6\big|_{v^6=T/R}.

The displayed coefficient formulas make A_4,B_6 homogeneous polynomials of degrees four and six in (R,T), over \mathbb C[u,w]. JacobianModel uses the integral equation

432V=432Y^2-432X^3-54\,\mathtt{a8}\,X-\mathtt{b256}, \qquad \mathtt{a8}=8A_4,\quad \mathtt{b256}=432B_6.

The historical identifier b256 is retained as an interface name; its scale here is 432. PhysicalJacobianCoefficients binds these coefficients to the physical cubic.

Over \mathbb F_5, specialize (u,w)=(2,1). The polynomial curve

\begin{aligned} R(T)&=1+3T+2T^2,\\ X(T)&=3+T+T^2+2T^3+2T^4,\\ Y(T)&=2+4T+T^2+3T^4+4T^5+4T^6 \end{aligned}

satisfies the equation. For general polynomials of these degrees, fixing R(0) and X(0) leaves thirteen coefficients. Equating the coefficients of T^0,\ldots,T^{12} gives thirteen equations. The Jacobian in the coefficient order

(R_1,R_2,X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4,Y_0,Y_1,\ldots,Y_6)

has determinant 1 for V, and determinant 2 for the integral equation 432V, modulo five. The latter factor is 432^{13}=2^{13}=2 in \mathbb F_5.

The polynomial source and its physical transport

The source needed here is polynomial:

P_J=\frac{uw}{144}(P_4X+P_6),

where

\begin{aligned} P_4={}&(R+w^6T) \left[\frac92(R+T)(R+u^6T)-\frac{u^4}{12}RT\right],\\ P_6={}&\frac{u^3w^2}{8}R^2T(R+T)(R+w^6T). \end{aligned}

Indeed, P_4=R^3\dot I_4, P_6=R^5\dot I_6, and hence on the physical field

P_J=\frac{uwR^5}{144}(\dot I_4\xi+\dot I_6).

JacobianSource applies the polynomial-source primitive theorem to this exact expression.

For tangent derivations D_i, write the residue divergence with normal h as

\operatorname{Div}_h(z) =\sum_i\left(D_i z_i-z_i\frac{D_i h}{h}\right).

Because P_J is independent of Y, the Jacobian primitive has divergence P_J/(2Y^2) with normal 2Y. PhysicalJacobianMap constructs the field embedding and proves compatibility of all tangent derivations. The residue-volume multiplier for this coordinate change is

\frac{F_{X_0}}{2Y}\det M=-144Rv^3.

The change-of-density Piola identity therefore pulls the Jacobian primitive back to a vector whose physical residue divergence is

-uwv^3\frac{\dot I_4\xi+\dot I_6}{2\eta^2}.

Two specified corrections complete the transport. First, PhysicalCubicVolume constructs a divergence-free physical vector \mathcal V whose action on rational functions is uwv^3gD. Adding

\frac{\dot\xi}{6\eta}\,\mathcal V

accounts for the derivative term in the moving-map identity.

Second, changing from the physical X_0-normal to the paired cubic normal produces the identity

\frac{A_{X_0}}{F_{X_0}} -\frac{AF_{X_0X_0}}{F_{X_0}^2} =uwv^3\frac{\dot g}{g}+\operatorname{Div}_{F_{X_0}}(\mathcal C).

PhysicalNormalTransport proves it explicitly. In the passive coordinate order (X_1,X_2,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2), the only nonzero component of \mathcal C is its Y_0-component,

-\frac{(X_1X_2Y_1Y_2)X_0Y_0^2} {X_0F_{X_0}+Y_0F_{Y_0}}.

Subtracting this vector gives the required physical residue-divergence equation. PhysicalPrimitive assembles the pulled-back primitive, the moving-map correction, and the normal-chart correction with these signs.
