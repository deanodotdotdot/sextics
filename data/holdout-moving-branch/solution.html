A transitive Jacobian primitive for the moving branch

Construction and scope

The extra term X_0^2\cdot Y_0^2\cdot Y_2^2/7 breaks the Fermat-cover exchange used for the central holdout. It preserves the paired signs, however, and its squared-coordinate cubic belongs to the transitive family of fqlx's mixed-biweight construction. This proof applies that method to the new coefficient functions and supplies a new nonsingular polynomial-curve seed. It does not introduce a new general geometric mechanism.

Set

R=X_2^2,\qquad v=Y_0/X_0,\qquad c_i=\frac{Y_i/X_i}{v},\qquad x=X_0^2/R,\quad y=X_1^2/R.

The exact sextic satisfies F=R^3U(x,y,1), where

U=ax^3+by^3+cz^3+dx^2y+ey^2z+fx^2z,
(a,b,c)=(1+v^6,1+c_1^6v^6,1+c_2^6v^6),\qquad (d,e,f)=(c_1v^2,c_1c_2v^2,c_2^2v^4/7).

The target is A=v^3R^3x^3. PhysicalCoordinates establishes the equation and the legality of its coordinate denominators.

The maintained transitive-cubic library supplies exact covariants H,\Theta,J. On U=0, the coordinates

\xi=\Theta/H^2,\qquad \eta=-J/(2H^3)

satisfy \eta^2=\xi^3+s_4\xi-s_6/4 and the invariant-differential identity D_y\xi/(2\eta)=3/U_x. Nonvanishing is proved on an explicit localization by evaluation at a point, rather than assumed for an arbitrary rational expression.

Polynomial curves on the Jacobian

With T=Rv^6, X=R^2\xi, and Y=R^3\eta, write

V=Y^2-X^3-A_4(R,T)X-B_6(R,T),\qquad A_4=R^4s_4,\quad B_6=-R^6s_6/4.

The coefficients are polynomials of degrees four and six in (R,T). JacobianModel uses the integral equation 111132V=0, with coefficient names a8 and b256 retained for compatibility: they mean 4116A_4 and 111132B_6, respectively.

At c_1=c_2=1, the following polynomial curve lies on this equation modulo five:

\begin{aligned} R(T)&=1+2T^2,\\ X(T)&=4+4T+T^2+3T^3+T^4,\\ Y(T)&=1+3T^2+4T^4+2T^6. \end{aligned}

Fixing the endpoint coefficients R(0)=1, X(0)=4 leaves thirteen unknown coefficients. Their thirteen incidence equations have an invertible Jacobian over \mathbb F_5. JacobianSeed supplies an exact inverse; JacobianSeedBinding identifies that matrix with the derivatives of the actual incidence equations. The modular identity alone would not suffice without this nonsingularity.

Arithmetic lifting retains c_1,c_2,R(0),X(0) as independent parameters and yields a solution over a finite extension of their generic field. Independent endpoints, the curve parameter, and a polynomial normal provide the dominant family needed for polynomial integration. Normalized trace descends the resulting primitive. The construction thereby integrates polynomial sources on this specified Jacobian model; it makes no assertion about arbitrary rational differentials on an elliptic curve.

Transport and certificate

Vary a=\alpha+v^6 at \alpha=1. The source -V_\alpha/(144R) is polynomial and is bound exactly in JacobianSource. PhysicalJacobianMap pulls its primitive back with residue-volume multiplier -144Rv^3. The moving-map and normal-chart corrections in PhysicalPrimitive then recover the prescribed physical target.

Irreducibility, coordinate nonvanishing, finite descent, legal denominator clearing, and homogeneous extraction give a nonzero homogeneous D, six numerators of degree \deg D+1, and a polynomial Q, with D relatively prime to F, satisfying

AD^2-D\sum_iN_iF_i+ F\left(D\sum_i\partial_iN_i-\sum_iN_i\partial_iD\right)=F^2Q.

Solution binds this existential certificate to the immutable target. No expanded witness or denominator-degree bound is exported.
