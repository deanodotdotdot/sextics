Over six complex variables X_0,X_1,X_2,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2, consider a smooth sextic fourfold X\subset\mathbb P^5 defined by a homogeneous sextic polynomial F(X_0,X_1,X_2,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2) invariant under the involution

\iota:(X_0,X_1,X_2,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2)\mapsto(X_0,X_1,X_2,-Y_0,-Y_1,-Y_2).

The involution \iota induces an action on X, and so also on H^4(X,\mathbb Q). As noted by Voisin, the Hodge substructure H\subset H^4(X,\mathbb Q) fixed by the induced action \iota^* has Hodge coniveau 1. Indeed, \iota^* acts as -1 on the nowhere-vanishing Calabi–Yau form \omega\in H^{4,0}(X).

The generalized Hodge conjecture thus predicts that H’s geometric coniveau should also be 1; that is, there should exist a divisor Y\subset X such that

H\subset\ker\left(H^4(X,\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow H^4(X\setminus Y,\mathbb Q)\right).

This particular instance of the GHC is interesting in its own right. Further, as Voisin points out, if true for general smooth even F, it would imply Voisin’s conjecture for K3 surfaces that are double covers of \mathbb P^2 ramified over a sextic curve.

Coordinates in the atlas. In these coordinates, “even” and “odd” mean even and odd total degree in the three Y variables.

In a recent work, Diamond shows that the prediction of the GHC as regards H\subset H^4(X,\mathbb Q) is equivalent to a purely algebraic one. For F given as above—an even homogeneous sextic whose vanishing locus is smooth—is it true that, for each q\in\{1,2\} and each odd monomial A of degree 6\cdot q, there exists a vector field v=(v_0,\ldots,v_5) whose components are homogeneous rational functions of total degree 6\cdot q-5, and whose common denominator D is not divisible by F, such that

F^{q+1}\;\bigm|\;A-q\cdot\mathrm dF(v)+F\cdot\operatorname{div}(v)?

Divisibility is interpreted in \mathbb C[X_0,X_1,X_2,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2]_{(F)}.

This latter condition is explicit, and proposed certificates can be checked computationally. Moreover, it has been solved for many instances (F,q,A). The goal of this autoresearch initiative is to finish the job, with the aid of an open, distributed effort.
